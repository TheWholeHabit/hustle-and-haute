Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Hustle & Haute: Empire Simulator is a free browser and mobile simulation game built with privacy by design.

1. Zero Data Collection

We do not require user accounts, logins, emails, or personal identifiers. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data.

2. Local Device Storage Only

Your game progress and theme preferences are stored exclusively on your own device using browser local storage. You can erase this data at any time using the in-game reset button or by clearing your browser/app storage.

3. No Ads or Third-Party Trackers

This application contains zero advertisements, zero analytics trackers, and zero third-party SDKs.
