📖 How to Play & Build Your Empire

Grow your Net Worth (Cash + Vault − Debt) to $500,000+ before Day 180 ends, unlocking all 4 chapters of your custom character story.

1. Buy Low on the Market Tab Look for green LOW tags where oversupply crashed prices. Tap +1 or Max to fill your inventory up to your Capacity limit.
2. Travel to Advance Days & Flip High Go to the Travel tab and pick a new hub. Each trip advances 1 Day and refreshes prices. Higher-tier hubs pay up to 1.35x more! Sell when you see red SPIKE tags or prices above your Avg cost.
3. Pay Off Starter Debt Immediately Your starter loan compounds daily based on your chosen Difficulty (2% Easy, 4% Normal, 7% Difficult). Open Bank and tap Pay Off Debt with your first profits.
4. Compound in the Vault & Edit Look Anytime Deposit spare cash into the Vault to earn daily yield, buy Pro Upgrades for extra capacity, and tweak your character's outfit, accessories, or name anytime in Settings!
